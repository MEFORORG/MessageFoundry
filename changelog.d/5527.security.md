- **The engine AI broker now honours the `[tls]` trust-anchor policy, and `[tls].crl_file` is a
  way across its revocation refusal.** The broker dialled one shared opener on the OS trust store,
  so in the shipped code `[tls].internal_ca_file` did not reach the `[ai].endpoint` hop and
  `[tls].crl_file` was never checked on it: under `pinned` the endpoint would still have been
  verified against every public root, and a revoked certificate would have been accepted wherever
  the hop crossed on warn or on its attestation. `POST /ai/chat` now hands the broker the
  instance `[tls]` policy, and an endpoint whose host is not loopback is verified against the
  internal CA under `augment` or `pinned` and checked against `[tls].crl_file` in any mode, on an
  opener of its own. With that CRL an off-box endpoint crosses `enforce` with no attestation, and
  the refusal text now offers `[tls].crl_file` beside `[ai].endpoint_revocation_attested`. The CRL
  is instance-wide: once it is set, an endpoint whose issuer has no CRL in that file fails its
  handshake, attested or not. See
  [CONFIGURATION.md](../docs/CONFIGURATION.md#tls--outbound-client-trust-anchors).
