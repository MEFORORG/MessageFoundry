- **A reload that fails or is cancelled before its outbound reconcile changes a lane now leaves
  every outbound lane as it was.** The rollback restored the registry and intake but left each
  outbound lane the reconcile had reached as the refused graph had it: its connector, retry,
  simulate and pause state, until a restart. A reload now builds every outbound connector before
  it changes any lane, then makes every lane change with no await, and closes the connectors it
  takes out of a lane only once its swap commits, even if the rest of the commit raises or is
  cancelled. A connector a not-deployed, `auto_start` or DR park retired, and that failed to
  close, rolled the reload back; it is now reported as a close failure, as a rebuilt lane's is.
  The rollback's sandbox close and inbound stops no longer stop it partway. The reload's lane CA
  check judges an inbound schedule window at the time its build check and listener loop share.
  The builds for the lanes a DR activation takes out of the park run together under one 30-second
  bound, and a lane whose build and startup check have not finished by then fails with a timeout.
  When the default executor refuses a worker-thread hop, at least some trust-anchor reads, a
  reload's target check, its content and reload snapshots of the config directory and its approval
  check, a DICOM listener's start and stop, and the sandbox session closes now run on a thread of
  their own, or on the event loop when no thread can start, with a WARNING, rather than failing at
  that hop or stopping the rollback. A DICOM listener's start still fails when no thread can start,
  since its accept loop needs one. Other hops still fail on such an executor, at least the config
  load, the environment value read, the bundle fingerprint, which also reads the config directory,
  and some connectors' startup checks. Such a hop still fails an operator reload, and an inbound
  whose restart in the rollback meets one stays down after the rollback, which still tries to
  restart the rest. Some trust-anchor reads outside a reload have no such fallback either, at least
  the SMTP alert CA check at serve start and the SQL Server store's certificate pin checks at store
  open. The sandbox close no longer drops its sessions unclosed when that hop is refused or its
  caller is cancelled.
