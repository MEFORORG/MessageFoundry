- **A reload that fails or is cancelled while it reconciles the outbound lanes now puts those
  lanes back.** The rollback restored the registry and intake but left each outbound lane it had
  reached as the refused graph had it: its connector, retry, simulate and pause state, until a
  restart. A reload now closes the connectors it takes out of a lane only once its swap commits, so
  a rollback puts each lane's own connector back open. A connector a not-deployed, `auto_start` or
  DR park retired, and that failed to close, rolled the reload back; it is now reported as a close
  failure, as a rebuilt lane's is. The reload's lane CA check judges an inbound schedule window at
  the time its build check and listener loop share.
