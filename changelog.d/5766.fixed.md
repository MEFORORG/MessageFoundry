- **A resolved alert's re-alert cooldown ends with it.** An auto-resolve, or an operator's resolve,
  now also drops the `NotifierAlertSink` cooldown for that alert, so a recurrence inside the
  cooldown pages and dispatches its rule's control action as a new incident. As before for
  `connection_started`, a resolve that fails leaves the row open and brings the cooldown back.
