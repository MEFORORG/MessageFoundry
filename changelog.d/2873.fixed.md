- **`extract_obx_document` refuses a message whose delimiters cannot be read as
  `BinaryCarriageError`.** On an `MSH-2` shorter than four encoding characters, or a body with no
  `MSH`, it used to let out a bare `ValueError` that quoted `MSH-1` and `MSH-2`; it now raises its
  documented error, naming the cause without quoting either field. A body with a short `MSH-2` is
  still accepted at the listener's parse; a body with no `MSH` is still refused there.
  (`BACKLOG #2873`)
