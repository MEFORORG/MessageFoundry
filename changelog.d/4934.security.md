- **`rotate-key` now opens its rotation record, when it opens one, from a new `open_store` hook,
  `before_first_write`, which a writable open runs before its first write under the store key.** A
  read-only open does not run it.
  **`serve` can now refuse to start on a changed store key the keyed audit chain cannot follow.**
  `[secret_rotation].allow_lost_audit_range_key = true` starts anyway and is reported as a security
  loosening; its entry in `docs/SECURITY-LOOSENING.md` states when the refusal fires and the way
  out. At least the `admin-*` commands, `issue-enrolment-code` and `provision-admin` do not refuse.
  (`BACKLOG #4934`, `BACKLOG #4936`)
