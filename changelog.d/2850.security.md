- **The `[backup]` and `[dr]` URL refusals now quote no part of the value.** The refusals of
  `[backup].destination`, `[dr].seed_archive`, `[dr].seed_dir` and `[dr].restore_token` printed the
  text in front of `://` as the scheme, and that text is whatever the setting held there. They now
  name the setting and say it is not a URL. This reverses the scheme quote added under
  `BACKLOG #2600`. Any scheme of two or more characters is still refused, and a one-letter scheme
  still reads as a drive letter. (`BACKLOG #2850`)
