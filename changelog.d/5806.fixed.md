- **The batch split drops the envelope trailer lines that end a message.** A `BTS`, `FTS`, `BHS`
  or `FHS` line that ends an `MSH`-led message, followed right after the id by that message's own
  field separator, is dropped from that message. Every other cut is the one the split made
  before. A one-message file that a whole-file source hands over as its own bytes loses those
  lines only under a UTF-8, ASCII, ISO 8859 or Windows-125x charset; under any other charset it
  goes over whole, as before. (`BACKLOG #5806`)
- **The dry run splits where byte order marks lead a later `MSH`**, as the live split of a UTF-8
  decode does. Under a single-byte charset the live sources do not cut there. (`BACKLOG #5918`)
- **The base64 content sniff reads only up to the prefix it decodes**, and a prefix that is not
  strict base64 now gives no signature. (`BACKLOG #5805`)
