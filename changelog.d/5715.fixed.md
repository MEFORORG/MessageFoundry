- **An HTTP inbound with `reply_from` now writes its `503` with `Retry-After` before it closes on
  stop or HA demotion.** `stop()` woke the turns blocked on a captured reply and then closed every
  client writer at once, so a woken turn wrote its `503` to a closing transport and the caller
  would have seen a bare connection close. `stop()` now waits for those turns to write their
  answers before it closes the writers, inside the same shutdown grace the client tasks already
  had, not on top of it. (`BACKLOG #5715`)
