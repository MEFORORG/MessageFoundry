- **A credential a generic ODBC connection string sends without braces is now hidden whole in a
  log line.** A password sent plain to a driver the engine has not measured, and every
  `odbc_params` value, `sslpassword` among them, may hold a space, `,`, `&` or, for a password,
  `=`. The log scrubbers stopped at the first three, so an echoed `;PWD=...;` would have printed
  the tail of the password, and `;sslpassword=...;` would have printed whole, since `ssl` glued to
  `password` gave the label no word boundary. A member right after a `;` whose key ends in a
  credential word, or in `token` (the builder's own secret keywords), such as `AccessToken`, now
  has its value taken to the next `;`, quote or line break, on the write-time log filters and in
  the support bundle and `GET /logs/tail`. It is a pass of its own that runs after every other
  credential pass, and in the bundle after the PHI pass, so it only ever hides more of a line than
  before. The costs, at least: text after a glued `;password=` in prose or a URL's matrix
  parameters is hidden to the next `;`, quote or the end of the line, and a later label inside
  that span loses its label to the placeholder. What it leaves, at least: a credential that is the
  FIRST member, with no `;` before it, still prints its tail after a space; a key that holds a
  credential word but does not end in one, such as `ClientSecretValue`, is read as before; and on
  the write-time chain a member glued into the same token as a percent-encoded HL7 run is taken
  with that run by the PHI pass first, so its tail after a space prints, as it did before.
