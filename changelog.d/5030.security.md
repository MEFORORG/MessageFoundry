- **No letter or digit of a credential a generic ODBC connection string sends without braces
  reaches a log line any more.** A password sent plain to a driver the engine has not measured, and
  every `odbc_params` value, `sslpassword` among them, may hold a space, `,`, `&` or, for a
  password, `=`. The log scrubbers stopped at the first three, so an echoed `;PWD=...;` would have
  printed the tail of the password, and `;sslpassword=...;` would have printed whole, since `ssl`
  glued to `password` gave the label no word boundary. A member right after a `;` whose key ends in
  a credential word or in `token`, such as `AccessToken`, now has every word of its value, to the
  next `;`, quote or line break, replaced by `redacted`, on the write-time log filters and in the
  support bundle and `GET /logs/tail`. It is a pass of its own that runs last of the credential
  passes. The value's spaces and punctuation stay where they were, because the same text is
  redacted again: a record two or three handlers filter, a log line the bundle reads back, and store
  error text. A draft that replaced the whole value took a separator, label, segment ID or base64
  length a later pass reads, and printed a value the scrubbers hid before on 124 to 145 lines of a
  320,000-line fuzz on each of those paths; the word mask prints none on 640,000 lines, those
  320,000 among them, on one pass or three. The costs, at least: a password's punctuation and word
  count print, though none of its letters or digits; a word that holds a credential, query or PHI anchor word, such as
  `MyPassword1`, prints, as does a segment ID before a separator; and text after a glued
  `;password=` in prose or a URL's matrix parameters is masked to the next `;`, quote or the end of
  the line. What it leaves, at least: a credential that is the FIRST member, with no `;` before it,
  still prints its tail after a space; a key that holds a credential word but does not end in one,
  such as `ClientSecretValue`, is read as before; and on the write-time chain a member glued into
  the same token as a percent-encoded HL7 run is taken with that run by the PHI pass first, so its
  tail after a space prints, as it did before. (BACKLOG #5030)
