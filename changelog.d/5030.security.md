- **A credential a generic ODBC connection string sends without braces is masked word by word in a
  log line, at least where its key ends in a credential word and follows a `;`.** A password sent
  plain to a driver the engine has not measured, and every `odbc_params` value, `sslpassword` among
  them, may hold a space, `,`, `&` or, for a password, `=`. The log scrubbers stopped at the first
  three, so an echoed `;PWD=...;` would have printed the tail of the password, and
  `;sslpassword=...;` would have printed whole, since `ssl` glued to `password` gave the label no
  word boundary. A member right after a `;` whose key ends in a credential word or in `token`, such
  as `AccessToken`, now has each word of its value, to the next `;`, quote or line break, replaced
  by `redacted`, except the words listed below, on the write-time log filters and in the support
  bundle and `GET /logs/tail`. It is a pass of its own that runs last of the credential passes. The
  value's spaces and punctuation stay where they were, because the same text is redacted again: a
  record two or three handlers filter, a log line the bundle reads back, and store error text. A
  draft that replaced the whole value took a separator, label, segment ID or base64 length a later
  pass reads, and printed a value the scrubbers hid before on 124 to 145 lines of a 320,000-line
  fuzz on each of those paths; the word mask prints none on 640,000 lines, those 320,000 among them,
  on one pass or three. What still prints, at least: the value's spaces and punctuation, and so its
  word count; a word that holds a credential, query or PHI anchor word where a later pass reads it
  as a label, such as the `pass` of `pass=word`; a segment ID before a `|`; a credential that is the
  FIRST member, with no `;` before it, whose tail after a space prints as before; a key that holds a
  credential word but does not end in one, such as `ClientSecretValue`; the text after a `;` inside
  a quoted value; and, on the write-time chain, a member glued into the same token as a
  percent-encoded HL7 run, which the PHI pass takes first, so its tail after a space prints as
  before. The cost, at least: text after a glued `;password=` in prose or a URL's matrix parameters
  is masked to the next `;`, quote or the end of the line. (BACKLOG #5030)
