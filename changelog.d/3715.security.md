- **A sign-out that races an elevation would now end the session under its new token.** An
  elevation in another tab re-keys the session, so a sign-out in flight used to match nothing and
  leave the session live while the user was told they had signed out. The sign-out now follows the
  rotation's link, as the sign-in supersession does. `POST /auth/logout` follows only links written
  after its request arrived; `/ui/logout`, which carries no credential, follows links written up to
  5 seconds before the call. That is a new, end-only power of a retired token, stated in
  [SECURITY.md](../docs/SECURITY.md). The `auth.logout` row then names the session's owner, where a
  console sign-out kept a NULL actor, and a `presented` field holds the hash that was sent.
  (`BACKLOG #3715`, `BACKLOG #3339`, `BACKLOG #3749`)
- **A sign-out that ends a lapsed session is recorded as `lapsed`, not `revoked`.** The row still
  names the owner, so the user sees it in their security-events feed. (`BACKLOG #3749`)
- **`auth.logout` rows for a sign-out that ended nothing are bounded.** At most 10 per client
  address and 100 in all per 60 seconds; past that the row is dropped and counted in an
  `auth.logout_rows_dropped` row. The sign-out itself is never throttled, and one that ended a
  session always writes its row. (`BACKLOG #3749`)
- **Once a sign-out ended its session, a failed audit row is logged rather than raised.**
  `/ui/logout` used to answer 500 then, without clearing the cookie or sending Clear-Site-Data.
  (`BACKLOG #3884`)
