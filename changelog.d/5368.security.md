- **Uploaded logs also refuse a ZIP record signature anywhere in the body, as defence in depth.**
  An appended ZIP or DOCX was already refused: every archive a ZIP reader extracts carries NUL
  bytes, and the whole-body NUL check (`BACKLOG #5551`) answers it 415. The new check also refuses
  a NUL-free byte run carrying a ZIP record signature. (`BACKLOG #5368`)
