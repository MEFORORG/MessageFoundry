- **A passkey sign-in that meets a store fault in its passkey re-read would now be refused with an
  audit row.** The account's passkeys are read again after the sign-count write and before the
  factor is marked. A store fault there used to raise out of the ceremony with no
  `auth.webauthn_failed` row. It is now logged and refused, the factor stays unmarked, and the
  row names `reason=store_error`. If the store refuses that row too, the refusal still comes
  back. A defect, such as a bad statement, still raises. Other store calls on the same path, at
  least the session, account and lock re-read just before it, are not covered. (`BACKLOG #3775`)
