- **A passkey sign-in that meets a store fault after its sign count moves would now be refused
  with an audit row.** The re-read of the session, account and lock, and the re-read of the
  account's passkeys, run after the sign-count write and before the factor is marked. A store
  fault in either used to raise out of the ceremony with no `auth.webauthn_failed` row, so the
  attempt left no trace beyond the moved counter. It is now refused, the factor stays unmarked,
  and the row names `reason=store_error`; if the store refuses that row too, the refusal still
  comes back and the failure is logged. (`BACKLOG #3775`)
