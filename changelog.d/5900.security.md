- **A correct re-auth password on a session the ceremony loses no longer clears the account's
  lockout counters or raises a login-after-failures notice.** The causes covered include at least
  a session revoked under the ceremony, one whose stamp fails its seal, and one whose rotation
  fails. Each answers `session_lost`, and the counters now stay as the failures set them. On a
  kept session the clear waits for the rotation, re-reads the account, and is skipped when its
  lockout state changed since the proof. Only writers that take the account's re-proof lock are
  kept out of the gap from that re-read to the clear: the sign-in leg, the second-factor legs and other engine shards take no such lock,
  and a failure or lock one of them writes between the re-read and the clear is still cleared. An
  account record that fails its integrity seal at that point is reported as a seal failure, its
  rotated session is ended, and the re-auth grants nothing. (`BACKLOG #5900`)
