- **A correct re-auth password on a session the ceremony loses no longer clears the account's
  lockout counters or raises a login-after-failures notice.** The causes covered include at least
  a session revoked under the ceremony, one whose stamp fails its seal, and one whose rotation
  fails. Each answers `session_lost`, and the counters now stay as the failures set them. On a
  kept session the clear waits for the rotation, re-reads the account, and is skipped when its
  lockout state changed since the proof. The gap from that re-read to the clear is held under a
  per-process lock that at least the sign-in leg's and the second-factor legs' lockout writes do
  not take, and that no other engine shard sees, so a failure or lock one of them writes there is
  still cleared. An account record that fails its integrity seal, or that Vault transit cannot
  check, at that point is reported, the store is asked to end the rotated session, and the
  re-auth grants nothing. (`BACKLOG #5900`)
