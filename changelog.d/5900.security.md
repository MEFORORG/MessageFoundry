- **A correct re-auth password on a session the ceremony loses no longer clears the account's
  lockout counters or raises a login-after-failures notice.** The causes covered include at least
  a session revoked under the ceremony, one whose stamp fails its seal, and one whose rotation
  fails. Each answers `session_lost`, and the counters now stay as the failures set them. On a
  kept session the clear waits for the rotation, and it is skipped when another leg changed the
  account's lockout state in the meantime. (`BACKLOG #5900`)
