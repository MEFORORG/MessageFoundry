- **An `anon.toml` overlay that points a default-scrubbed field at a different surrogate kind is
  refused unless it lists that field under `[hl7].retarget_acknowledged`.** Such a retarget can
  leave part of the value in the dataset while the leak-check counts the field as scrubbed. It
  used to load with a warning. A retarget to the same kind, to `drop` or to `freetext` needs no
  entry, and an entry that names no such retarget is refused. A listed retarget still warns.
  See [PHI.md](../docs/PHI.md) section 9. (`BACKLOG #3000`)
- **`anonymize` and `anonymize_checked` take no `overlay` path.** Load it once with `load_rules`
  and pass `rules=` to each message. They used to read and parse the overlay for every message,
  and a process-wide record of warnings hid the overlay warnings from every later load. That
  record is gone: `load_rules` warns on every call. An overlay field number has at most three
  digits, and the overlay's file path is printed with unprintable characters escaped.
  (`BACKLOG #3493`)
