- **A passkey registration finish can no longer bind past a factor gained on another session
  during the finish.** The insert is now conditional: the store asks the factor-binding rule again
  inside the insert's own transaction, with the account row locked, so a TOTP or passkey bound on
  another session after the service's own checks is refused with `400` `this account already has
  a second factor`, audited `auth.mfa_denied`, with no passkey stored and the session not stamped
  MFA-verified. A session already stamped MFA-verified still adds a passkey beside its factors, and
  an account with no factor still binds its first one. The TOTP confirm keeps its read-then-act
  window. See [SECURITY.md](../docs/SECURITY.md). (vault `BACKLOG #3014`, `#3690`)
