- **App-log compression keeps a log that changed while it was being compressed.** The retention
  runner now pins the file it reads (file id, size, mtime and ctime, taken on the read handle before
  and after the read and checked against the scan's mtime) and checks the path again before placing
  the archive and before removing the original. It does not rename over an archive that appeared
  since the scan. Where the platform has `O_NOFOLLOW` and `O_NONBLOCK` (not Windows), it also no
  longer follows a symlink or blocks on a FIFO at the path. A log appended to, rewritten, or rotated
  into the same path during compression is no longer deleted with it in those cases; it is left in
  place and not counted as compressed. When the change lands after the archive is placed, the
  archive stays beside the log, and without an `app_log_days` window that path is not compressed
  again until an operator removes the archive. The checks and the acts they gate are still not
  atomic: at least a change landing between the last check and the remove, and a concurrent
  compressor renaming its archive over another's in that window, can still lose data.
  (`BACKLOG #6805`)
