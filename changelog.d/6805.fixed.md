- **App-log compression keeps a log that changed while it was being compressed.** The retention
  runner now pins the file it reads (file id, size and times, checked before and after the read and
  against the scan's mtime) and checks the path again before placing the archive and before removing
  the original. A log appended to, rewritten, or rotated into the same path during compression is no
  longer deleted with it; it is left in place and not counted as compressed. The check and the remove
  are still not atomic: at least a change landing between the last check and the remove, and a
  concurrent compressor renaming its archive over another's in that same window, can still lose
  data. (`BACKLOG #6805`)
