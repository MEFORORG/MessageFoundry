- **Every directory sign-in refusal records the client address.** The OIDC `state_unknown`,
  `state_mismatch` and claims-check refusals, and the other OIDC and Windows SSO refusals that share
  their audit path, wrote `auth.login_failed` with no `client`. So a spray of forged callbacks or
  junk tokens showed no source. Each row now records the caller's address, as the `token_refused`
  row already did, and a NULL `client` again means no client was in scope. (vault BACKLOG #2132)
