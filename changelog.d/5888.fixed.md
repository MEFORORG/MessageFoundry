- **A DR backup, restore-verify or restore no longer stalls the engine on a slow read-only key
  provider, and a DR activation's takeover timeout can now fire on one.** The `auto`, `env`,
  `dpapi` and `vault` providers resolve in their own thread. The timeout ends the wait, not that
  thread: it runs until the provider returns, and holds process exit until then. The first key
  resolve under the `host` provider stays on the event loop, because it can convert and delete a
  custody handoff; a full verify can still resolve that key again in its worker thread. A scheduled backup whose provider never returns still records no failure and raises no
  alert. (`BACKLOG #5888`)
