- **A DR activation's restore-verify no longer stalls the engine on a slow read-only key provider,
  and its takeover timeout can now fire on one.** Under the `auto`, `env`, `dpapi` and `vault`
  providers, `restore-verify` resolves the keyring in its own thread. The timeout ends the wait,
  not that thread: it runs until the provider returns, and holds a shared pool worker and process
  exit until then. Under the `host` provider the keyring still resolves on the event loop, because
  that resolve can convert and delete a custody handoff, so no timeout can fire on it. `restore`
  and a scheduled or on-demand backup also still resolve their key on the event loop.
  (`BACKLOG #5888`)
