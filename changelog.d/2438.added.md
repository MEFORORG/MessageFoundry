- **The audit trail, a user's security events and the connection event log page past their
  first window.** `GET /audit` and `GET /me/security-events` take `offset` and return `total`,
  `limit` and `offset` beside their rows. `GET /events` takes `offset` and states its total in an
  `X-Total-Count` header; its body is still a bare list. Each total is counted under the same
  filters as its page: the lock rows a reader without `users:manage` cannot see are left out of
  the total too, and a channel-scoped caller's event total counts only its own events. The store's
  `list_audit`, `list_connection_events` and `security_events_for_user` take `offset` on all three
  backends, `count_audit` takes `limit=None` for an uncapped count, and `count_connection_events`
  and `count_security_events_for_user` are new. No schema change. (`BACKLOG #2438`)
