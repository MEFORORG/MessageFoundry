- **The anonymizer's leak-check and site-code scrub read escaped and IPv6 values.** In both the
  toolkit and the tee: the MRN-typed check reads an `MR` or `MRN` id type in any component, whole
  or as its first subcomponent, as before, and now also in any subcomponent of CX.5, each as
  written or decoded and trimmed of whitespace and control characters, while an `MR` in a later
  subcomponent outside CX.5, such as `PET&MR`, passes; a routable IPv6 address is refused, while at least the loopback, link-local,
  unique-local and documentation ranges pass, and text such as `Note::` or a colon-written EUI-64
  is refused though it holds no address (PHI.md says which); the site-code check and
  scrub find a site code joined to an escaped delimiter or written with a hex escape, and the
  scrub replaces it, escapes and all, with digits of the same width. In a part that holds an
  escape character, a site-code run that whitespace sets apart also counts, and the scrub then
  replaces the whole part, free text included, with digits; and the check for a shape cut
  across fields reads each field decoded too. See
  [PHI.md](../docs/PHI.md), *What the leak-check refuses, and what it lets through*.
