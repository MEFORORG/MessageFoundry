- **Every session can now be ended at once, and a restored store ends them on its first start.**
  `DELETE /sessions` ends every account's sessions and keeps only the caller's own. It needs
  `users:manage` and a single-use re-authentication bound to `revoke_all_sessions`.
  `messagefoundry admin-revoke-sessions` does the same from the host, for when the API is not
  trusted. Both write an `auth.session_revoked` audit row with the count. `messagefoundry restore`
  now ends every session in the store it places and marks it, and the first `serve` on that store
  revokes every session again, writes
  a `store_restored` audit row naming the archive's SHA-256, its age and its newest audit row, and
  raises a `store_restored` alert. `restore` refuses an archive older than 7 days, or one that does
  not say when it was taken, unless `--allow-old-archive` is given or `--max-age-days` is raised. It
  prints the archive's newest audit time, because every disable, delete, role change and reset made
  after it must be re-applied by hand. See [SECURITY.md](../docs/SECURITY.md) and the restore
  section of [EARLY-ADOPTER-GUIDE.md](../docs/EARLY-ADOPTER-GUIDE.md). (`BACKLOG #2614`)
