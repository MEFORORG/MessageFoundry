- **A traced dry-run no longer keeps Message writes it will never emit.** Once an invocation hit
  the 5,000-event cap, `dryrun --trace` stopped emitting lines but went on holding every later
  `msg[...]` write in memory, so a long mutating loop grew without bound. Writes are now dropped
  past that cap, and also past a separate per-invocation cap of 5,000 writes, which bounds the
  writes a Handler's helper makes on a single traced line. Either cap marks the invocation
  `truncated`. (`BACKLOG #6824`)
