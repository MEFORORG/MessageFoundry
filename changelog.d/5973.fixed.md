- **The tray reads an engine on a wildcard `serve --host` bind as up.** The self-signed
  certificate the engine mints for a `0.0.0.0` or `::` bind also names the loopback of its family,
  the address the tray dials for that bind, so the pinned probe verifies it. An operator
  certificate on a wildcard bind must name that loopback too. (`BACKLOG #5973`)
