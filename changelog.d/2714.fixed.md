- **The auth rate limiter drops a key's aged bucket even when that key never returns.** Before this
  fix, `SlidingWindowRateLimiter` removed a key's bucket only inside a later call for that key. A
  limiter keyed on a client or peer address would have kept a bucket for every address it saw. It
  would have held them for the life of the process. Now the limiter orders its table by each key's
  newest hit. Each call drops up to two buckets from the front whose newest hit has left the
  window. The table never grows past the most keys live at one time, and no single call scans it.
  A key that is still being refused keeps its bucket, so `Retry-After` is unchanged. A limiter with
  no per-key gate keeps no buckets at all. (`BACKLOG #2714`)
