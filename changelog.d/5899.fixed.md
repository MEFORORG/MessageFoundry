- **`connections.toml` policy tables refuse an unknown key and a wrong-typed value.** An unknown
  key in `[outbound.retry]`, `[outbound.buildup]` or `[outbound.stall]` was dropped with the default
  kept, so a typo could leave the stall alert off or the retry cap unchanged with no error. Those
  tables and `[outbound.batch]` also coerced a quoted number, a boolean, or a float where an integer
  is declared. Both now fail the load. An unknown key in any policy table, `schedule` included, is
  named without its value. `max_attempts = "forever"` still loads. (`BACKLOG #5899`)
