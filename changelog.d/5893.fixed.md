- **Stopping one `reply_from` HTTP listener no longer drains its siblings.** Every such listener
  shared one reply rendezvous, so stopping any one of them woke every other listener's waiting
  turns with `shutting_down` and left the rendezvous draining, and every later synchronous-reply
  turn on every listener resolved `degraded` without waiting for its reply until the engine
  restarted. Each listener start now drains
  only its own turns, and the next start of a stopped listener waits for replies again. Delivery
  hints still reach a waiter whichever listener armed it, and the waiter cap stays engine-wide.
  [ADR 0154](../docs/adr/0154-synchronous-captured-downstream-reply-and-intake-authentication-for-the-inbound-http-listener-adr-0023-deferred-tail.md)
  records the change. (`BACKLOG #5893`)
