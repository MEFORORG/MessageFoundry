- **A store open that fails or is cancelled closes everything it opened, and settles the AES-GCM
  invocation block it reserved.** On PostgreSQL and SQL Server the failed-open cleanup now also
  runs on a cancel, and goes through the store's own close, so the block reserved at open is
  settled rather than forfeited, except where the open failed to bind the store's salt and the
  cipher may count for another store. On SQLite a read-pool connection is tracked before its first
  PRAGMA, so a PRAGMA that fails no longer leaves its worker thread running. On all three backends
  the cleanup runs to completion even when a cancel arrives while it runs, and that cancel is
  raised once it is done. (`BACKLOG #5720`)
