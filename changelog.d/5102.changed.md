- **An operator-only fault at intake is a configuration refusal, answered `503` with no
  `Retry-After`.** At least an inbound `encoding` that sets up no decode is classed so. It refuses
  every body until an operator edits the connection, so `Http()` no longer tells a sender to re-send
  in 5 seconds; it answers `503` alone, and the `ERROR` row's error text opens
  `[refusal kind: configuration]`. The DICOM C-STORE SCP answers it Out of Resources (`0xA700`), and
  MLLP and TCP still answer `AE`. A file or remote-file source still records the `ERROR` row and
  consumes its input, which that row stores whole. The decision is ADR 0154's amendment of
  2026-11-04. (`BACKLOG #5102`)
