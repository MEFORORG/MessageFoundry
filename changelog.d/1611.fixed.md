- **A row whose re-pend fails after a store fault is recovered without a restart, and no later row
  on its lane overtakes it.** A store fault between a committed claim and its handoff leaves the
  row `inflight`, and the worker's re-pend of it can fail too. Before, that re-pend was tried once,
  and the lane went on claiming past the stranded row until the next start. Now a per-lane worker,
  or a pooled lane serializer, whose re-pend fails retries it on a backoff that doubles from 1 s to
  30 s while it holds the lane, claiming nothing newer until the row is pending again, so per-lane
  order holds. The first re-pend write is always sent; a retry is sent only while this node is the
  leader. A pooled lane held that way raises one `lane_stuck` alert at `infra_fault_stop_after`
  failures. The trade: a re-pend that keeps failing now holds its lane, where before the lane
  moved on out of order; the `lane_stuck` alert (pooled) and the in-flight watch added in 0.5.0
  page such a lane. At least these are not covered. A stop during the retry leaves the row in
  flight for the next start, as before. A worker killed by an error that is not an `Exception`
  still leaves its row in flight until the next start, and its replacement claims past it, as
  before; in the pooled claim mode a serializer killed that way is not respawned. A claim that
  committed but whose reply never reached the worker leaves it no record of the row, which stays
  in flight until a store recovery path re-pends it, at least the next start. (`BACKLOG #1611`)
