- **A passkey counts as a way past the sign-in lock only under the configured relying party.**
  The factor-drop gates (TOTP removal, passkey removal, the rotation gate, the store's conditional
  writes behind them) and the lockable-account census count a discoverable passkey only under
  `[api].public_origin`'s host, since the sign-in accepts only a passkey under the request's
  `rp_id`. With `public_origin` unset they count none, so a covered account keeps its TOTP rather
  than drop it for a passkey that might not sign it in. `messagefoundry verify` reads the same
  host. (`BACKLOG #3937`)
- **A passkey sign-in costs one unit of the sign-in limiter, not two.** `POST /ui/login` pops the
  staged challenge before its own charge and skips the charge only when it found one; a forged or
  absent handle is charged. The seal refusal's `auth.login_failed` row says `combined` only where
  the sign-in was one. (`BACKLOG #3937`)
