- **`[auth].password_min_length` may not exceed 1024, the longest password the sign-in, step-up
  and password-change requests accept.** A larger floor is refused when the settings load. Before,
  it loaded, and the engine minted temporary passwords at least that long that no sign-in surface
  would accept. The bound is one constant, `PASSWORD_MAX` in `messagefoundry/config/settings.py`,
  which the request models now read. (`BACKLOG #5397`)
- **The engine refuses an enrolment code longer than 64 characters where a binding step reads it,
  as a refused code, without normalising or hashing it.** The TOTP enrolment confirm and the
  passkey registration both read it there, after every refusal the service gave before that read.
  The JSON `MfaConfirmRequest` bounds it at the same 64 characters. (`BACKLOG #5399`)
