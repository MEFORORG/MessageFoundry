- **A fault in the inbound handler on the raw-TCP and X12 listeners is recorded as
  `handler_error`, and a sender that waits for no reply no longer loses frames to it.** Both
  listeners caught the fault in their last-resort arm, recorded `framing_error` though the frame had
  been read cleanly, and dropped the connection. A sender that waits for no reply never resends, so
  that would have lost the frame and every frame already sent behind it. Now an HL7 inbound with
  acknowledgements on closes with no reply, as before, so its sender resends. Any other inbound
  holds the frame and retries the handler, backing off to one attempt every 30 seconds, until it is
  recorded or the listener stops; the sender is stalled meanwhile rather than losing frames, and
  only a stop loses the one frame held. A fault after the commit can leave a copy per attempt, which
  at-least-once delivery allows. An `OSError` raised by the handler is now `handler_error` too, not
  `peer_reset`. (`BACKLOG #5022`)
