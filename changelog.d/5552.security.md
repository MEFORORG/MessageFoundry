- **Uploaded logs: the text-only check reads the whole body, links are not followed, and a sidecar
  refusal is bounded and reported.** The 415 check on POST `/uploads` now reads every byte of the
  body rather than a leading sample, so a NUL byte or a control-dense 8 KiB window anywhere refuses
  it. What it still lets through is stated in
  [CONNECTIONS.md](../docs/CONNECTIONS.md), under the uploaded-logs "Permitted types". An upload
  whose body or sidecar is a symbolic link or a junction is not followed by a read, a delete or the
  retention prune, and a sidecar that is a link or not a regular file is not opened by the listing
  scan; such a pair is left for an operator to remove, and is logged once per upload per hour
  rather than on every pass. A sidecar refusal no longer logs an unbounded part of the stored text,
  and a sidecar that fails its integrity tag under a key the store holds raises the cipher's
  tag-failure alert, once per upload per hour, counted only once an alert hook is armed.
  (`BACKLOG #5551`, `BACKLOG #5552`, `BACKLOG #5553`)
