- **A DR backup, restore-verify and restore count the real snapshot when a staging path holds `#`,
  `?` or `%`.** The row counts and `integrity_check` opened the snapshot through a bare `file:`
  URI, which SQLite cuts at a `#` or `?` and decodes at a `%`. With such a character anywhere in a
  staging path, at least the store's data directory, the OS temp directory or the restore
  destination's directory, the open could drop `mode=ro` and read an empty database it created at
  the cut path. The backup then recorded no row counts in its manifest, and a verify of that
  archive compared nothing and passed. Both checks now open the snapshot read-only through the
  store's own URI builder, as the full verify's decrypt pass already did. A test refuses a new
  `file:` URI built by an f-string, `+`, `%` or `.format` in the engine's packages, outside the two
  builders that encode the path. (`BACKLOG #2186`)
