- **Queue buildup and stall alert thresholds load only when greater than zero.** `[delivery]`
  `buildup_max_depth` must be at least 1, and `buildup_max_oldest_seconds` and
  `stall_max_oldest_seconds` above 0. The per-outbound `buildup=` / `stall=` thresholds, and their
  `[outbound.buildup]` / `[outbound.stall]` tables in `connections.toml`, take the same bounds. NaN
  and a boolean are refused too. A zero or negative threshold was crossed by every lane holding a
  message, NaN was never crossed, and a boolean was read as 1. Infinity still loads: an infinite age
  is never crossed, so it keeps a default-on age dimension from firing. (`BACKLOG #5778`)
