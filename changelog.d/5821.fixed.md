- **`copy_field` and `split_field` no longer escape a whole field's escapes a second time.** A
  whole-field source copied or split into a component or subcomponent keeps its escape sequences
  as sent and has its separators escaped, so a field sent as `O\S\Brien` reads back as `O^Brien`
  there. That write now raises `ValueError` on field 1 or 2 of `MSH`, `BHS` or `FHS`. A
  whole-field destination still takes the raw text as it is. (`BACKLOG #5821`)
