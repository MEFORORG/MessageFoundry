- **`copy_field` and `split_field` no longer escape a whole field's escapes a second time.** A
  whole-field source copied or split into a component or subcomponent is written there as one
  leaf that reads back as the field's leaves, each decoded, so a field sent as `O\S\Brien` reads
  back as `O^Brien` there. A whole-field destination still takes the raw text as it is. Both now
  raise `ValueError` on a component or subcomponent destination in field 1 or 2 of `MSH`, `BHS` or
  `FHS`, whatever the source. (`BACKLOG #5821`)
