- **A refused `FhirLookup` no longer stops the engine from starting.** Before this, one lookup
  whose hop was refused at start, such as an off-box https read with no CRL and no revocation
  attestation under `enforcement = enforce`, unwound the whole start. On a clustered node the
  graph never started, and the supervisor logged "will retry" on every pass. A refused lookup is
  now isolated like a lane, on every start. It is recorded failed, logged at ERROR and alerted as
  `connection_stopped` under `fhir_lookup:<name>`. Every Handler call to it raises
  `FhirLookupError` before anything is sent, so its messages take the error path and are never
  sent on without the lookup. The other feeds start. `messagefoundry check`, dry-run and reload
  still refuse the whole config while the lookup is refused. A reload or a restart with the cause
  fixed brings it back. A refused `tls_ca_file` on a lookup is a separate check and still refuses
  the start.
  (`BACKLOG #3008`)
