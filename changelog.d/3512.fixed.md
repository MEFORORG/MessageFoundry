- **A CRL expiry alert about a CA-file CRL no longer claims more than the engine proved.** The
  engine may be unable to confirm the signature of a CRL now in a hop's CA file. The alert's
  `detail` then asks you to check the CRL and then restart. It used to say that every handshake
  would fail, and handshakes can still pass in that case. For a CRL proven badly signed, `detail`
  now uses a start's words, and says the CRL may be removed when a good one from that issuer is
  already in the file. A file can be one hop's CRL setting and another hop's CA file. There, a
  CA-file fix no longer drops a refused reload's remedy: `detail` gives it after the fix. The
  certificate monitor also keeps a CRL row's alert when it cannot work out which rows share the
  file, and logs that as an error. (`BACKLOG #3512`)
