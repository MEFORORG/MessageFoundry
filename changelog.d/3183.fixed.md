- **The `upload-cipher` alert and the store cipher's refusal of a plaintext upload no longer
  promise that `rotate-key` will seal it.** Both, and the listing scan's log line that carries the
  cipher's text, now offer `rotate-key` with the engine stopped, and say that an upload it leaves
  unsealed for good can only be deleted, by an operator or by the retention prune. A per-uploader
  quota refusal (HTTP 409, and its `upload.reject_quota` audit reason) now says how many of the
  files it counts cannot be read and are not listed, and how many bytes they hold, whenever those
  files count toward the cap that refused it. It names no file. (`BACKLOG #3183`)
