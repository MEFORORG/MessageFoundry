- **A captured reply whose body does not decrypt would no longer hold its loopback lane.** In the
  shipped code, a reply owed to a `Loopback()` inbound whose stored body failed to decrypt (a
  tampered or misplaced cell, a key that was not supplied at start, a corrupt marker) raised out
  of the re-ingress step, and the worker handed the token back to the head of the lane on every
  pass. No later reply on that loopback would then have re-ingressed. The re-ingress step now
  dead-letters that token at once, as a claim already does an unopenable payload: the token keeps
  its reference, so it can be replayed once the key is supplied, the origin message finalizes
  `ERROR` with a `dead` event, and the lane moves on to the next reply. (`BACKLOG #4995`)
