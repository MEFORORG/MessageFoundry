- **The self-signed certificate the engine generates for a loopback bind names 127.0.0.1, ::1
  and localhost.** A pair the engine mints, renews or re-mints for a changed bind, for a
  `127.0.0.1`, `::1` or `localhost` bind, now names all three, so a client on the engine's machine
  verifies it at whichever of the three it dials, where the engine listens at that address. A
  wildcard bind still names itself and its own family's loopback, and any other bind names itself.
  A renewal or re-mint also keeps the loopback names docs/SERVICE.md lists. The pair never adds
  the machine's host name beyond the bind host. A pair that already names the address a client
  dials for the bind is reused as found: after a move from `0.0.0.0` to `127.0.0.1` it names
  neither `::1` nor `localhost` until its renewal. (`BACKLOG #5790`)
