- **The self-signed certificate the engine generates for a loopback bind names 127.0.0.1, ::1 and
  localhost.** A pair the engine mints, renews or re-mints for a bind the engine treats as
  loopback (`127.0.0.1`, `::1`, `localhost`) names all three, so a client on the engine's machine
  verifies it at whichever of the three it dials, where the engine listens at that address. A
  wildcard bind still names itself and its own family's loopback, and any other bind names itself.
  A renewal or re-mint names the current bind's names, plus, for a bind a client dials through
  `127.0.0.1`, `::1` or `localhost` (at least a wildcard, or another letter case or IPv6 spelling
  of one of those), each of those three that the old pair named and the bind does not itself name
  (compared without regard to case or a trailing dot), and always `127.0.0.1`; no other old name
  is kept. The pair never adds the machine's host name beyond the bind host. A reused pair that
  lacks a name the current bind requires, such as one minted for `0.0.0.0` after a move to
  `127.0.0.1`, or one minted before this change, is re-minted at the next start, with the audit
  reason `bind_changed`; docs/SERVICE.md states which names a bind requires. (`BACKLOG #5790`)
