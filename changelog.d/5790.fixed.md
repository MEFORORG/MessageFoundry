- **The self-signed certificate the engine generates for a loopback bind names 127.0.0.1, ::1
  and localhost.** A pair the engine mints, renews or re-mints for a bind on any spelling of
  `127.0.0.1`, `::1` or `localhost` names all three, so a client on the engine's machine verifies
  it at whichever of the three it dials, where the engine listens at that address. A wildcard
  bind still names itself and its own family's loopback, and any other bind names itself. A
  renewal or re-mint also keeps the loopback names docs/SERVICE.md lists. The pair never adds the
  machine's host name beyond the bind host. A reused pair that lacks a name
  the current bind requires, such as one minted for `0.0.0.0` after a move to `127.0.0.1`, or one
  minted before this change, is re-minted at the next start, with the audit reason
  `bind_changed`; docs/SERVICE.md states which names a bind requires. (`BACKLOG #5790`)
