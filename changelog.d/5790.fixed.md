- **The self-signed certificate the engine generates for a loopback or wildcard bind names every
  loopback name.** A pair the engine mints or renews for such a bind now names `127.0.0.1`, `::1`
  and `localhost` beside the bind host, so a client on the engine's machine verifies it at
  whichever of the three it dials, where the engine listens at that address. A bind to one
  specific off-loopback address names that address alone. It never adds the machine's host name
  beyond the bind host. (`BACKLOG #5790`)
