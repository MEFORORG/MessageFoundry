- **The self-signed certificate the engine generates for a loopback bind names 127.0.0.1, ::1 and
  localhost.** A pair the engine mints, renews or re-mints for a bind the engine treats as
  loopback (`127.0.0.1`, `::1`, `localhost`) names all three, so a client on the engine's machine
  verifies it at whichever of the three it dials, where the engine listens at that address. A
  first-run mint for a wildcard bind still names itself and its own family's loopback, and for any
  other bind names itself alone. The pair never adds the machine's host name beyond the bind host.
  A reused pair for a loopback bind that lacks any of the three, such as one minted for `0.0.0.0`
  after a move to `127.0.0.1` or one minted before this change, is replaced at the next start,
  audited with reason `bind_changed`, or `renewal` when it is also due. (`BACKLOG #5790`)
