- **The self-signed certificate the engine generates for a loopback bind names 127.0.0.1, ::1 and
  localhost.** A pair the engine mints, renews or re-mints for a bind the engine treats as
  loopback (`127.0.0.1`, `::1`, `localhost`) names all three, so a client on the engine's machine
  verifies it at whichever of the three it dials, where the engine listens at that address. A
  first-run mint for a wildcard bind names itself and its own family's loopback, and for any other
  bind names itself alone; docs/SERVICE.md says what a renewal or re-mint keeps. The pair never
  adds the machine's host name beyond the bind host. A reused pair for a loopback bind that lacks
  any of the three, such as one minted for `0.0.0.0` after a move to `127.0.0.1` or one minted
  before this change, is replaced at the next start, with at least the exceptions docs/SERVICE.md
  lists, audited with reason `bind_changed`, or `renewal` when it is also due. (`BACKLOG #5790`)
