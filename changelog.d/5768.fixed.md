- **One arrival on an idle lane no longer raises a saturation alert.** The saturation window's rise
  must now reach `[delivery].saturation_min_growth` messages (default 5, from 1 to 1000000) before
  it fires. Depth samples alone cannot tell one arrival on an idle lane from slow growth, so the
  floor is what keeps `[0, 0, 0, 1]` from paging, while a lane that grows past it, flat steps
  included, still pages. (`BACKLOG #5768`)
