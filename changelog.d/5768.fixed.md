- **One arrival on an idle lane no longer raises a saturation alert.** A lane's backlog must now
  rise by `[delivery].saturation_min_growth` messages (default 5, from 1 to 1000000) since it last
  fell before `saturation` fires. A lane that keeps growing past that, flat steps and slow growth
  included, still pages. `saturation_sustain_samples` now refuses a value below 2, or a boolean,
  at load. (`BACKLOG #5768`)
