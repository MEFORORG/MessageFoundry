- **One arrival on an idle lane no longer raises a saturation alert.** `SaturationDetector` now
  fires only when its window never falls, rises at least twice, and spans a positive time, so a
  primed idle lane taking one message, or one tick's burst, no longer raises `saturation`. Flat
  steps between rises still count, so a lane sampled faster than its arrivals still fires.
