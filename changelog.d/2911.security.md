- **The email address rule now checks the shape of the domain, not just its characters.** It
  refuses an empty label, a dot at either end of the domain, a label that starts or ends with a
  hyphen, and a label longer than 63 characters. It also refuses a domain whose last label is all
  digits, such as a bare IP address: SMTP writes an IP address only in brackets, and the bracketed
  form was already refused. Before this, `a@example.org.`, `a@example..org` and `a@10.0.0.1` would
  have passed on a first deployment. The rule covers the `Email()` and `Direct()` sender and
  recipients, the `[alerts]` email sender and recipients, and per-user notification addresses. A
  trailing-dot recipient never matched `[egress].allowed_recipient_domains`, so that check already
  refused it, under a less useful reason. (vault BACKLOG #2911)
