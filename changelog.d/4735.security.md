- **`/ui/logout` audit rows for sign-outs that end no session are now bounded.** The route needs no
  credential, so any caller that could reach it could append `auth.logout` rows with `outcome`
  `nothing_to_revoke` without limit. Each source now writes at most 3 such rows in any 60 seconds,
  and all sources together at most 120. A sign-out past either limit is counted in one row a
  minute, with `outcome` `unrecorded` and `"count": N`. A sign-out that ends a session, or that names its caller as
  `POST /auth/logout` does, always writes its row.
  [SECURITY.md](../docs/SECURITY.md#session-inventory--targeted-revocation-wp-10) states the
  bound. (`BACKLOG #4735`, `BACKLOG #3749`)
