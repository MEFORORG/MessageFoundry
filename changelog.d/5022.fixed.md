- **The MLLP listener no longer drops a frame its inbound handler faulted on when the inbound sends
  no reply.** On an inbound with acknowledgements off, or one that is not HL7 v2, it recorded
  `handler_error` and read on, so a sender that never resends would have lost that frame. Now it
  holds the frame and retries the handler, as the raw-TCP and X12 listeners do: backing off to one
  attempt every 30 seconds until the frame is recorded or the listener stops, with the frames
  behind it kept in order and the sender stalled meanwhile. A fault the first retries clear logs
  WARNING lines and records no event; `handler_error` and one ERROR line mark a fault still failing
  after four attempts. A stop during the hold gets one last attempt; if that fails too, the held
  frame and every frame behind it on that connection are lost, and one ERROR line says so. A sender
  the `tls_client_subjects` allowlist refuses is not held: its frame gets one attempt and its
  connection closes after the read, as before. An inbound that replies in HL7 still answers `AE`
  (`CE` in enhanced mode) and closes. (`BACKLOG #5022`)
