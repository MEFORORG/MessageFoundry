- **Every connector reads its `port` by one rule, and a TCP delimiter byte by the count rule.** An
  uncast `env()` hands a connector the environment's value as it stands, and a bare `int()` read a
  `true` as port 1 and `2575.9` as port 2575. A bool, a fraction, text that is not a whole number and
  a number outside 0 to 65535 are now refused when the connector is built, so at wiring and in
  `messagefoundry check`, naming the setting and withholding the value. `start`, `end` and `trailer`
  on `Tcp()` take the same reading, so a whole float such as `2.0` now passes there and the refusal
  no longer quotes the value. (`BACKLOG #1650`)
