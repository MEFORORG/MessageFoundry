- **Your own user page in the web console no longer offers Save roles, Save channel scope or Sign
  out all sessions.** Each of those writes ends the target account's sessions, so each refuses your
  own account (`BACKLOG #1149`), and the page offered a button that could only fail. It now shows a
  note in place of each, and points to your account page for your sessions. The other account
  actions on that page are not changed by this entry. A channel-scope post aimed at your own
  account is now refused, and written to the audit log as `auth.self_target_refused`, before the
  form is checked. Before, a malformed one was answered by the form error and left no audit row.
  (`BACKLOG #3393`)
