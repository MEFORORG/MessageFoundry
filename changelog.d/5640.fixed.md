- **The two-box shardcert drives now count every outbound copy and poll the engine box over
  https.** `shardcert-driver` used to pass a broadcast run that lost most of its outbound copies,
  because its loss check counted distinct messages only. Its verdict now also checks that the sink
  received the accepted intake times the fan-out that SHARDS_READY reports: exactly that on a no-kill
  run, at least that across a kill. `shardcert-driver`, `shardcert-drive` and
  `shardcert-drive-ladder` now poll the engine box's API over https by default, signed in as the
  rig Administrator, and take `--cacert` for the engine box's certificate. They sign in before any
  load is offered, and a refusal exits 2 with a hint. `--insecure` keeps the plaintext poll with no
  credential.
