- **A lane's trust-anchor refusal is worded by one rule, and its advice is no longer cut.** A
  connection's status and its `connection_stopped` alert keep the whole fixed line, so the advice
  at its end survives a long connection name. Only the lane check's own setting refusal, such as a
  blank `tls_ca_pin` or a CA path holding a NUL, is told to fix the setting; any other refusal
  is a file to fix. A DR activation that a trust anchor refuses names the setting and connection
  it refused wherever the refusal carries them, at least for a lane's CA, still with no part of
  the CA's path. A config reload refused by a trust anchor, or stopped by the store, further down
  the cause chain is audited with reason `trust_anchor` or `store_unavailable`. A refused CRL
  file is named by one spelling of its path, an absolute one first, whatever order the engine met
  its hops in. (`BACKLOG #3498`)
