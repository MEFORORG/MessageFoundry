- **A lane's trust-anchor refusal is worded by one rule, and its advice is no longer cut.** A
  connection's status and its `connection_stopped` alert keep the whole fixed line, so the advice
  at its end survives a long connection name. Only the lane check's own setting refusal, such as a
  blank `tls_ca_pin`, is told to fix the setting; any other refusal is a file to fix. A DR
  activation that a trust anchor refuses now names the connection and setting it refused, still
  with no part of the CA's path. A config reload refused by a trust anchor further down the cause
  chain is audited with reason `trust_anchor`. A refused CRL file is named by one spelling of its
  path, whatever order the engine met its hops in. (`BACKLOG #3498`)
