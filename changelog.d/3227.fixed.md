- **Engine shard names are no longer scrubbed out of three log lines and refusals.** The log's
  PHI pass reads two capitalized words around a comma as a possible patient name. Engine shards
  named in capitals or in Title case, such as `EAST` and `WEST`, were joined with a comma, so the
  log showed `[redacted]` where the names belonged. That hid which engine shards were down after a
  crash loop. It did the same in the refusal of a multi-shard config on a single-file store, and
  in the refusal of a reload that changes the engine shard set. All three now join the names with
  `/`, as in `EAST/WEST`. Lower-case names were never affected. The Windows fix text for a
  writable trust anchor also lost the words `Authenticated Users`; it now says
  `Authenticated users`, which Windows accepts. The redaction itself is unchanged.
  (`BACKLOG #3227`)
