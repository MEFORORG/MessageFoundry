- **Engine shard names are no longer scrubbed out of three log lines and refusals.** The log's
  PHI pass reads two capitalized words around a comma as a possible patient name. The engine
  joined engine shard names with a comma. Names in capitals or in Title case, such as `EAST` and
  `WEST`, then showed as `[redacted]` in the log. That hid which engine shards were down after a
  crash loop. It also hid the names in two refusals: a config with more than one engine shard on
  a single-file store, and a reload that changes the engine shard set. All three now join the
  names with ` / `, as in `EAST / WEST`. Lower-case names were never affected. A name the pass
  scrubs by itself, such as `East Wing`, is still scrubbed. The Windows fix text for a writable
  trust anchor also lost the words `Authenticated Users`. It now says `Authenticated users`,
  which Windows accepts. The redaction itself is unchanged. (`BACKLOG #3227`)
