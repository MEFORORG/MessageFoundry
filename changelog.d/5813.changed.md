- **A log record the sink's formatter cannot format is replaced by a notice, where the formatter
  can format one.** Before this change such a record rolled the sink and, when its re-write failed too, went to
  stage 2. Now, when the
  sink takes a fixed notice naming the record's logger and level in its place, the engine keeps
  processing: only that record's content is replaced by the notice, and a rate-limited stderr line
  is written. Nothing shows on `GET /status`. **This is weaker than before, and an owner ruling on
  it is pending.** A formatter that cannot format even that notice still records the sink
  unwritable. [ADR 0162](../docs/adr/0162-fail-closed-application-log-write-guard-detect-roll-and-stop.md)
  amendment C states the rule. (`BACKLOG #5813`)
