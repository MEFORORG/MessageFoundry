- **A log record the sink's formatter cannot format no longer stops the engine.** Before this
  change such a record rolled the sink and went to stage 2 of the application-log write guard,
  which asked for the stop when every sink was unwritable. Now, when the sink takes a fixed notice
  naming the record's logger and level in its place, the engine keeps processing: only that
  record's content is replaced by the notice, and a rate-limited stderr line is written. Nothing
  shows on `GET /status`. A formatter that cannot format even that notice still records the sink
  unwritable. [ADR 0162](../docs/adr/0162-fail-closed-application-log-write-guard-detect-roll-and-stop.md)
  amendment C states the rule. (`BACKLOG #5813`)
