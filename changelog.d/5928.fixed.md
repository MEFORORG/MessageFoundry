- **The text-transform actions now work on a field's decoded value and keep its escapes.**
  `convert_case`, `substring_field`, `pad_field` and `replace_literal` transformed a field's raw
  wire text, so they cased, sliced, counted and matched escape sequences as if they were data:
  upper-casing `\.br\` made `\.BR\`, which no decoder maps, a slice could cut `\S\` in half, and a
  leaf holding `\.br\` raised, because its decoded line break cannot be written back. They now
  transform only the literal text and keep every escape they do not cut as sent. `convert_case` and
  `replace_literal` work leaf by leaf and keep a whole field's components, repetitions and
  subcomponents. `replace_literal` writes `new` as data, so a `new` holding `^` no longer creates a
  component, and an `old` holding a separator matches only an escaped one inside one leaf.
  **BREAKING:** `substring_field` and `pad_field` now raise `ValueError` on a field or component
  that holds a component, repetition or subcomponent separator, where they cut or padded its raw
  text; address the one part instead, such as `PID-5.1`. All four raise `ValueError` on `MSH-1` and
  `MSH-2`. `copy_segment` now numbers occurrences as `Message` and `Peek` do, so a fieldless bare
  line such as `PID` is no occurrence, and a `count_segments` loop copies every segment it counts.
  (`BACKLOG #5928`)
