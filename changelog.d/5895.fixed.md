- **A synchronous HTTP `reply_from` turn now settles on the `reply_from` outbound's whole row set,
  once no other handler of the message is still being routed or transformed.** A dead or cancelled
  row no longer answers `502` while a sibling row or a sibling handler could still capture the
  reply, an unrelated outbound's retries never hold the answer, and a delivered row with no captured
  reply (shadow mode) answers as an empty reply (`204`) rather than `no_route` (ADR 0154 D3, amended
  2026-11-18). (`BACKLOG #5895`)
