- **The redactor no longer prints text inside a malformed XML end tag of a PHI element.** In a
  `<family>`, `<given>`, `<name>` or other vocabulary element, the words after an end tag's
  name, as in `</family Doe>` or a `</family Doe` that never got its `>`, were copied into the
  redacted output as they stood. They are now masked in place, letter for letter, keeping the text's
  length and layout, so text joined to a redacted message and redacted again hides at least what it
  hid before. Words next to punctuation, a credential label or a date are still kept as before.
  (`BACKLOG #5925`)
