- **The redactor masks a plain word inside a malformed XML end tag in or after a PHI element,
  where the XML pass sees the tag.** Inside a `<family>`, `<given>`, `<name>` or other vocabulary
  element, the words after an end tag's name, as in `</family Doe>` or a `</family Doe` that
  never got its `>`, were copied into the redacted output as they stood; so were those after a
  vocabulary end tag outside such an element. Where those words are letters, digits and spaces
  that the rules expect no other pass to read, they are now masked in place, letter for letter,
  keeping the text's length and layout. Everything else there is kept as before, at least: words
  next to punctuation, a tab or a non-ASCII letter, a credential label or a date, words on a later
  line of the tag, every such word in a text holding the credential backstop's opener, and at the
  very end of the text, a last word a following text could complete. An HL7 field run that takes
  the end tag's name still prints the words after it, as before, in at least these shapes: a run
  before the element (`a|b|<family>Smith</family Doe>`), one in its content
  (`<given>A^B^C</given Doe>`), and one before a stray end tag (`a|b|</family Doe>`).
  A mask alone does not send the text through the passes again: in a draft of this change it did,
  and that extra round printed a value joined after a redacted `</given Doe>&Smith, Jones=PID|x`
  that the earlier redactor hid. Masked text still reaches passes the earlier redactor ran on the
  unmasked text, at least the next round after one that masks and also scrubs, the widened second
  stage, and a later call; whether that prints such a value is measured below, not proved. Seeded differential tests compare each answer with the earlier
  redactor's, by `redact`, `safe_text`, the log handler's filters and at least six other routes,
  on generated text alone, joined to raw text, and joined to raw text after `redact`, the log
  handler's filters or `safe_exc`. Over 1,500 generated texts around vocabulary elements and end
  tags, each joined to a second generated text, 1,000 more that end on a shape like that `PID|`
  join, and 66 hand-built joins of that shape, every answer was the earlier one with some letters
  and digits masked, so none printed a value the earlier one hid. The first 1,500 did not catch
  the draft's extra round; the other two sets did. That is a measurement of those inputs, not a
  proof for every input. (`BACKLOG #5925`)
