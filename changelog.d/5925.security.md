- **The redactor no longer prints a plain word inside a malformed XML end tag of a PHI element.**
  For a `<family>`, `<given>`, `<name>` or other vocabulary element, the words after an end tag's
  name, as in `</family Doe>` or a `</family Doe` that never got its `>`, were copied into the
  redacted output as they stood. Where those words are letters and digits that no other redaction
  pass or credential filter reads, they are now masked in place, letter for letter, keeping the
  text's length and layout. Everything else there is kept as before, at least: words next to
  punctuation, a tab or a non-ASCII letter, a credential label or a date, words on a later line of
  the tag, and at the very end of the text, a last word a following text could complete. A seeded
  differential test, which also joins redacted text to raw text and redacts it again, found no
  value the earlier redactor hid that this one prints; that is a measurement, not a proof.
  (`BACKLOG #5925`)
