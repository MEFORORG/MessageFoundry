- **The redactor masks a plain word inside a malformed XML end tag in or after a PHI element,
  where the XML pass sees the tag.** Inside a `<family>`, `<given>`, `<name>` or other vocabulary
  element, the words after an end tag's name, as in `</family Doe>` or a `</family Doe` that
  never got its `>`, were copied into the redacted output as they stood; so were those after a
  vocabulary end tag outside such an element. Where those words are letters, digits and spaces
  whose masking the rules expect to change no pass's reading, they are now masked in place, each
  letter and digit to `X`, `x` or `0`, keeping the text's length and layout. Other such words are
  kept as before. The docstring of `_end_tag_rest` in `messagefoundry/redaction.py` lists, at
  least, the cases kept and the passes that still read masked text that the earlier redactor ran
  on unmasked text.
  A mask alone does not send the text through the passes again: in a draft of this change it did,
  and that extra round printed a value joined after a redacted `</given Doe>&Smith, Jones=PID|x`
  that the earlier redactor hid. Seeded differential tests compare each answer with a stand-in
  for the earlier redactor: this change's redactor with the mask and the round gate patched out,
  so every end-tag rest is copied as it stood and no change counts as a mask alone. They compare
  by `redact`, `safe_text`, the log handler's filters and at least six other routes, on generated
  text alone, with raw text appended, and with raw text appended after `redact`, the log
  handler's filters or `safe_exc`. Over 1,500 generated texts around vocabulary elements and end
  tags, each followed by a second generated text, 1,000 more that each end on one of five fixed
  shapes (three name pairs glued to a segment id, like that `PID|` join, and two JSON values), and
  66 hand-built joins of the `PID|` shape, every answer was the stand-in's with some letters and
  digits masked. The last two sets each check that they fail with the round gate patched out, as
  in the draft. That is a measurement of those inputs, not a proof for every input.
