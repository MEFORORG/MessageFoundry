- **The redactor masks a plain word inside a malformed XML end tag in or after a PHI element,
  where the XML pass sees the tag.** Inside a `<family>`, `<given>`, `<name>` or other vocabulary element, the words after
  an end tag's name, as in `</family Doe>` or a `</family Doe` that never got its `>`, were copied
  into the redacted output as they stood; so were those after a vocabulary end tag outside such an
  element. Where those words are letters, digits and spaces that the rules expect no other pass to
  read, they are now masked in place, letter for letter, keeping the text's length and layout.
  Everything else there is kept as before, at least: words next to punctuation, a tab or a
  non-ASCII letter, a credential label or a date, words on a later line of the tag, every such word
  in a text holding the credential backstop's opener, and at the very end of the text, a last word
  a following text could complete. An HL7 field run that takes the end tag's name, as in
  `a|b|<family>Smith</family Doe>`, still prints the words after it, as before. A seeded differential test, which also joins redacted text to
  raw text and redacts it again, found no value the earlier redactor hid that this one prints; that
  is a measurement, not a proof. (`BACKLOG #5925`)
