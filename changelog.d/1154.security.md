- **The live monitor socket and the bulk export check the caller again while they run.** The
  `/ws/stats` feed resolves the session before every frame instead of every 3 s, and now also
  checks the second factor and the notification address there, not only at the handshake. A
  refusal closes the socket before the next frame. `GET` and `POST /messages/export` resolve the
  caller before every row. A revoked session or a withdrawn permission records
  `messages_export.stopped` and aborts the download, and a narrowed channel scope skips the rows it
  no longer covers. On a first deployment, a revoked or narrowed grant would otherwise have kept
  receiving up to three more frames, or the rest of an export of up to 100,000 bodies.
  (`BACKLOG #1154`)
- **The VS Code extension's cached AI policy expires when it enables assistance.** When the engine
  is unreachable, a cached answer that enables assistance now stands for 12 hours from the engine's
  answer, then assistance turns off until the engine can confirm it. A cached answer that disables
  assistance still stands at any age. A cache from an earlier version counts as expired.
  (`BACKLOG #1154`)
