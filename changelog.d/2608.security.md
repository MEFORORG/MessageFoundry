- **A flood of refused inbound bodies no longer grows the store unmetered.** A body an inbound
  listener refuses before ingress, at least from decode to strict validation, is still recorded as an `ERROR` row, and
  the sender still gets its AR or AE. Its length and SHA-256 are now always stored, and the whole
  body only while its inbound's rolling allowance covers it. Past it, the first 8 KiB are stored and
  the row's error says so. The new `[inbound]` keys `refused_body_allowance_bytes` (default 32 MiB),
  `refused_body_window_seconds` (default 3600) and `refused_body_head_bytes` (default 8192) set it.
  It ships on and never pauses intake. See [CONFIGURATION.md](../docs/CONFIGURATION.md).
