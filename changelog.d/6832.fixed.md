- **A cut close of the security notifier records the notices it left unsent.** When the shutdown
  cut `SecurityEventNotifier.aclose` while a send waited on a slow relay, the notices still queued
  got no audit row, and on a full queue the drain ran on past the close. The close now cancels its
  drain and, before it returns, records the cut send with `reason: interrupted` and each queued
  notice with `reason: abandoned`, and logs how many were queued. At shutdown those writes run in
  the cut step's short grace, so a write still to run when it ends meets the closed store and is
  lost; [SECURITY.md](../docs/SECURITY.md) keeps that as an open residual. (`BACKLOG #6832`)
