- **Audit writes that share a transaction are now tested where the COMMIT itself fails.** A
  dual-control request, a dual-control transition and an administrator's TOTP replacement each
  append their audit row in the write's own transaction. Tests now refuse that COMMIT after the
  row was appended, on SQLite and SQL Server, and show the change rolls back with its row and that
  nothing is forwarded off-box. The TOTP replacement also gains a refused-append test on all three
  backends and a check that its committed row is forwarded once. The two INFO lines a repeat
  approval request writes are tested to escape a CR or LF in the requester's name. The
  `Executor` and `approve()` docstrings now say which executors' audit rows commit with the
  effect, and that a COMMIT whose reply was lost is still recorded as `failed`. No behaviour
  changed. (`BACKLOG #3324`)
