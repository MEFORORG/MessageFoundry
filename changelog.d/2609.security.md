- **Windows SSO refuses an account that holds a federated binding.** An account an administrator
  has bound to a federated identity now signs in through its identity provider only. `POST
  /auth/negotiate` and `GET /ui/sso` answer a bound account as they answer any failed sign-in, and
  the `auth.login_failed` audit row carries `reason=federated_sign_in_required`. The binding
  decides, not `oidc_enabled`, so unbind an account to give it Windows SSO back. A bound account
  has no Windows SSO fallback during an identity provider outage, so keep a local administrator
  account. A session Windows SSO minted before the bind is not revoked, and it cannot enrol the
  account's first engine factor. An account with no binding is unchanged. See
  [Federated sign-in](../docs/SECURITY.md#federated-sign-in-oidc-browser-only--adr-0142).
  ([BACKLOG #2609](../docs/BACKLOG.md))
