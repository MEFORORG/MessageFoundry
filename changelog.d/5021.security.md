- **A `connection_error` alert no longer hands its reason to any notifier transport.** The
  webhook and email notifier bodies carried the outbound failure's `safe_exc` reason, which can
  echo a partner's reply text. In its place they carry the exception class, or the fixed token
  `unnamed error` for a class that is not a plain identifier, beside at least the connection and
  the kind. The reason is still stored as the
  alert instance's masked, revealable reason. See [PHI.md](../docs/PHI.md) rows 10 and 11.
  (BACKLOG #5021)
