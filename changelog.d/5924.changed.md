- **The `tcp` and `tls` syslog forwarders send octet-counted frames.** Each record goes as
  `MSG-LEN SP <PRI>msg`, MSG-LEN being the number of bytes after the space: the `<PRI>`, the
  handler's ident (which the engine leaves empty) and the rendered record, UTF-8 encoded (RFC 6587,
  RFC 5425), not as a NUL-terminated line. A collector that splits a raw TCP stream on LF alone cannot parse either
  form, and one set to split on NUL, which parsed the old form, cannot parse the new one; point
  either at an octet-counting listener. `udp` is
  unchanged. See `forward_protocol` in [CONFIGURATION.md](../docs/CONFIGURATION.md). (`BACKLOG #5924`)
