- **A generic-ODBC hop now needs a TLS mode, not just a TLS-named keyword.** Only `sslmode` or
  `encrypt` with a value counts as the operator owning TLS on `dialect='generic'`. A lone `sslkey`,
  `sslcert`, `sslca`, `sslcapath` or `sslcipher`, or a blank mode, would have been read as ownership
  although a driver's own default need not require TLS. Such a hop now warns, is reported, and is
  refused off-loopback on an enforcing instance. A mode value outside the no-TLS deny-list stays
  delegated. [CONNECTIONS.md](../docs/CONNECTIONS.md) records the limits of that, for `encrypt` and
  for psqlODBC's spelling of a mode. (`BACKLOG #2483`)
