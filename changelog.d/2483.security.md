- **A generic-ODBC hop now needs a TLS mode, not just a TLS-named keyword.** Only `sslmode` or
  `encrypt` with a value counts as the operator owning TLS on `dialect='generic'`. A lone `sslkey`,
  `sslcert`, `sslca`, `sslcapath` or `sslcipher`, or a blank mode, would have been read as ownership
  although libpq defaults to `sslmode=prefer`. Such a hop now warns, is reported, and is refused
  off-loopback on an enforcing instance. A mode value outside the no-TLS deny-list stays delegated.
  `encrypt` is unproven on psqlODBC and on MySQL drivers, so
  [CONNECTIONS.md](../docs/CONNECTIONS.md) points those drivers at `SSLmode` or `SSLMODE`.
  (`BACKLOG #2483`)
