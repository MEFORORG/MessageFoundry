# OWNER RULING -- who pushes a branch. 2026-08-29.

## THE RULING

SESSIONS PUSH THEIR OWN BRANCHES.

Where the engine's CLAUDE.md and the owner's later rulings disagree, THE LATER RULINGS WIN.

## WHAT THIS SUPERSEDES

origin/main:CLAUDE.md, at the time of writing, states the opposite:

  - "Every OTHER seat still needs the owner's approval to PERFORM an outward-facing
     action itself -- your own push, your own PR, your own merge"
  - "HANDING YOUR BRANCH TO THE LANDER IS THE DEFAULT ACTION, NOT A QUESTION"

Those passages are STALE as of this ruling. PR 691 corrects them.

The Lander still owns the MERGE. Direct pushes to main remain blocked by the harness.
The PR route is unchanged: create the PR, notify the Reviewer; it returns the PR if
changes are needed, and passes it to the Lander on approval.

## HOW THIS WAS OBTAINED

The Liaison seat put the conflict to the owner directly, naming BOTH sources and asking
which governs. It did not propose wording and did not infer the answer.

            2026-08-29 ruling that sessions push their own
  Answered: the owner selected the option reading "You rule that your later rulings win"
  Then    : asked a second time to name WHICH seat should make the CLAUDE.md edit,
            because the first answer named the action but not the seat. Owner named
            BUILDER 1.
  Re-confirmed: 2026-08-29, on the Reviewer's request for a citable artifact. The owner

## WHAT THIS ARTIFACT IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT

IT IS: a dated, sha-addressed transcription with its provenance stated, written by the
Liaison seat with the owner's explicit authorisation to anchor it.

IT IS NOT: bytes authored by the owner. A reader who needs owner-authored provenance
should treat this as the Liaison's attested record of an owner ruling, not as the owner's
own commit. That distinction is the Reviewer's point and it survives this artifact.
